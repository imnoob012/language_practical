6-2-13 集計結果からの取得②
クエリ
select 
  a.account_id as "ユーザーID", a.name as "ユーザー名", sum(quantity*price) as "合計金額" 
from 
  order_item as oi 
left join item as i on i.item_id = oi.item_id 
left join order_info on order_info.order_info_id = order_id 
left join account as a on a.account_id = order_info.account_id
group by a.name, a.account_id 
order by a.account_id asc;


結果
  ユーザーID | ユーザー名 | 合計金額 
------------+------------+----------
          1 | ac1        |     4782
          2 | ac2        |    12050
(2 rows)
